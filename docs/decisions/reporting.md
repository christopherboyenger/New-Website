# Reporting (Organic) decisions

Decisions made where the specifications are silent or conflicting. Format: `ID — decision — rationale — spec reference`. Nothing here is claimed as observed reference behavior unless it says "observed".

- REPORTING-01 — Organic URL keys: observed `tab`, `campaign`, `from`, `to`, `comparisons`; proposed `playbook`, `platform`, `review`, `view`, `mode`, `metrics`, `chart`, `sort`, `order`, `page`, `q` (`search` accepted as an alias). Malformed values fall back to defaults, serialization is canonical (defaults omitted, dates always written), and every change except an explicit page change resets to page 1. Row selection, dialogs and popovers are local UI state, not URL state. — Shared links and Back/Forward must restore the view; only some keys were observed. — evidence audit item 8; build spec "Query-state proposal".
- REPORTING-02 — The default tab is Posts. — The bare Organic URL was observed showing the posts view. — L02.
- REPORTING-03 — Views, Spend and CPM in the Campaigns and Playbooks tables, and the range metric cards, come from `post_daily_metrics` of APPROVED posts for the UTC range. CPM is total spend ÷ total views (weighted), never an average of row CPMs; zero views shows "—", not $0.00. — No misleading numbers. — build spec §2 "Metric semantics"; DEC-011.
- REPORTING-04 — Table count rules: Posts = approved posts with at least one daily metric row in the range; Creators = their unique creators (deduplicated across the range); Avg Views/post = Views ÷ Posts ("—" without posts); Playbooks = every playbook attached to the campaign, drafts included (the Playbooks tab lists drafts too, so the numbers agree). — The spec does not define these counts. — L03/L04; production-ui-plan "Scope counts deliberately".
- REPORTING-05 — Tab counts: Campaigns = all workspace campaigns; Playbooks = all playbooks, or those of the filtered campaign; Posts = the posts the Posts list shows for the current range, mode and filters (all review statuses unless filtered; search excluded). — A tab badge should match what the tab shows. — L02–L04.
- REPORTING-06 — The Posts list shows all review statuses (In review, Approved, Rejected) with a badge and a review-status filter; metric cards and the chart count approved posts only. Unattributed posts are excluded from the list unless the Campaign filter is "Unattributed" (sentinel `0`); Custom posts are playbook sentinel `0`. — Brands need to see submissions under review; reporting counts approved content. — build spec §1 post drilldown sentinel rules.
- REPORTING-07 — Metric cards: Total Views, Spend, CPM, Posts, Creators are range values (earned in the UTC range) with a daily series. Likes, Comments, Bookmarks, Shares and Engagement % are LIFETIME counters of the posts in the list scope (mode + filters), labelled "Lifetime total"; they have no daily series, so charting one shows an explanation instead of a line. Engagement % = (likes+comments+bookmarks+shares) ÷ lifetime views over posts with both observed. Never-observed counters show "—". — Only lifetime counters are stored per post; daily values would be fabricated. — L33; build spec §2.
- REPORTING-08 — Comparison mode draws at most the 5 largest groups (by the charted metric; CPM ranks by spend) and says so. A data-table alternative is always available. — Readability and accessibility; the spec sets no limit. — L02 "Add comparison".
- REPORTING-09 — Bulk "Move" and "Review" (seen in the posts selection bar) are omitted. — They were never observed working and their semantics are unknown. — L02 snapshot.
- REPORTING-10 — Campaign Active switch: owners only (`campaign:manage`), shows the persisted status, and every change needs a confirmation. Pause explains it stops spending AND earning for all posts, including older ones; Activate explains visibility to eligible creators and that the daily budget is an average. Allowed: inactive→active, paused→active, active→paused. Activation re-checks plan entitlements of the campaign's settings. Members see a status badge. — The label "Active" is not proof of state; edits never activate. — evidence audit item 5; build spec §3; DEC-005, DEC-006.
- REPORTING-11 — Status and budget writes use optimistic concurrency (`expectedVersion`); a stale version returns a conflict with a Reload action. An unchanged budget is a no-op (no version bump, no audit row). Audit actions: `campaign.activate`, `campaign.pause`, `campaign.budget_update`, `playbook.activate`, `playbook.deactivate`. — No silent overwrite. — production-ui-plan "Concurrent edit".
- REPORTING-12 — "Edit budget" edits the Daily-mode average only (min $50, whole cents), says it is an average and not a cap, and shows the weekly figure (7 × daily). Manual-pricing campaigns show their budget read-only. Saving never changes status. — DEC-015. — L03 "Edit budget".
- REPORTING-13 — The playbook distribution switch exists only for published playbooks and requires `playbook:publish`; drafts show a "Draft" badge. Toggling doesn't bump the draft version (an open editor won't conflict) and is guarded by compare-and-set on `active`. — A pause is separate from draft/published status. — production-ui-plan; L04.
- REPORTING-14 — Breakdown shows a row per UTC day only for days with views or spend (sparse), plus range totals (weighted CPM, deduplicated posts/creators) with a note that totals can be lower than the sum of days. On the Campaigns tab it breaks down the selected campaigns, or all on the page when none are selected. — DEC-011. — L03/L04 "Breakdown".
- REPORTING-15 — Create Playbook: radio list "Name (Status)", Continue disabled until a choice is made; Continue creates a DRAFT via `createPlaybookDraft` (`playbook:edit`, so members may) and opens `/home/<slug>/playbooks/<id>/edit`. The filtered campaign is preselected. With no campaigns the dialog explains why and links owners to New Campaign. — Creating a draft never publishes or activates. — L23; production-ui-plan "Create-playbook dialog".
- REPORTING-16 — Header actions follow the observed placement: New Campaign on the Campaigns tab, Create Playbook on the Playbooks tab. A viewer whose role can't use one sees it disabled with the reason beside it. — "Disabled actions explain their prerequisite nearby". — L03/L04.
- REPORTING-17 — Freshness: each tab shows "Metrics last reported <viewer local time>" from the latest metrics observation. After 24 hours without a new observation, a warning says metrics may be out of date and the last values stay visible. Approved posts with no metrics ever reported show "Metrics aren't available yet" and "—" (never 0). — Honest stale/unavailable states. — production-ui-plan "Stale report/sync", "Metrics unavailable"; DEC-013.
- REPORTING-18 — Date range picker: popover with "All times in UTC", "<n> days selected", Quick select presets, two months (one under 640px), Reset to Last 30 Days, Cancel, Apply. Presets, Reset and day clicks change only the draft; Apply commits to the URL (push) and removes `page`; Cancel, Escape, an outside click or tabbing away discard. The first click starts a range (a single day is valid), the second completes it. Days after today (UTC) and spans over 366 days can't be picked. Keyboard: arrows, Home/End, PageUp/PageDown (Shift = year), Enter/Space. — Apply commits / Cancel discards is documented; whether Reset applies immediately was not observed. — L36; evidence audit item 8.
- REPORTING-19 — Post timestamps (`posted_at`) render in the viewer's local time zone (a UTC date until hydration); report days, chart days and ranges stay UTC. Posts without a thumbnail show a neutral "No thumbnail" placeholder. — "UTC aggregation and timestamp display timezone remain distinct." — production-ui-plan.
- REPORTING-20 — The Platform column shows "All platforms" when a campaign targets every platform (empty targeting list), and platform names otherwise, instead of brand logos. — No third-party brand imagery is bundled, and text is accessible. — L03/L04.
- REPORTING-21 — The Organic page renders its own header instead of `PageHeader`, because `PageHeader`'s actions container is `shrink-0` and overflowed horizontally at 390px with the date picker plus a create button. — No horizontal page scroll on phones. — CLAUDE.md mobile rules.
