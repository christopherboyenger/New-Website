# Creator portal and campaign admission — decisions

Format: `CREATOR-PORTAL-NN — decision — rationale — spec reference`. These are product decisions made for this build where the spec was silent or conflicting. They are not observed reference behavior: the reference creator app was never observed.

- CREATOR-PORTAL-01 — A creator profile (`creators.user_id`, unique) is attached to an ordinary user account. `/creator/sign-up` creates the login through the shared auth service and then the profile; a signed-in user without a profile (for example a brand teammate) gets a profile-only form. One person can be a brand member and a creator. Signed-out visits to `/creator/*` redirect (HTTP 307) to `/auth/sign-in?next=…`; signed-in users without a profile go to `/creator/sign-up`. The portal group has no `loading.tsx`, so the sign-in redirect is a real 307 and not a streamed client redirect. — One identity system and session model. — production-requirements.md §2 "Creator onboarding and fulfillment".
- CREATOR-PORTAL-02 — Country targeting: an empty country list means all countries; otherwise the creator's profile country must be listed. — Matches the campaign form's "empty = all" semantics. — reference-build-specification.md campaign targeting.
- CREATOR-PORTAL-03 — Platform targeting: the creator needs at least one linked account on a targeted platform (any platform when none are targeted). Submissions must come from such an account. — A creator cannot fulfil a campaign on a platform they don't post on. — production-requirements.md §2.
- CREATOR-PORTAL-04 — Minimum creator level compares level rank (bronze < silver < gold < platinum < diamond). "Dedicated account required" needs a linked account flagged dedicated **and** verified on a targeted platform. Because ownership verification isn't configured, such campaigns are closed to everyone for now, and the portal says so. — A self-reported handle is not proof of ownership. — production-requirements.md §2.
- CREATOR-PORTAL-05 — Campaigns with audience-demographic targeting (age, gender, metro) are not offered: creator profiles don't record audience demographics, so eligibility can't be evaluated. They are never assumed to match. — Honest states. — CLAUDE.md non-negotiable 2.
- CREATOR-PORTAL-06 — Discovery lists only `active` campaigns with at least one `published` + active playbook whose published revision exists. Campaigns from an org that removed the creator (`org_creators.removed_at`) are hidden and look like "not found". Campaign cards show the brand, primary hashtag, format and requirements. They show no payout rates, budgets or reach figures. — No rate model exists, and promises would be fabricated. — Task brief; CLAUDE.md non-negotiable 2.
- CREATOR-PORTAL-07 — Joining creates `campaign_memberships` with status `approved`, or `requested` when `approvals.requireCreatorApproval` is set, and upserts `org_creators`. Joins respect `maxCreators` (counted under a row lock). A creator who was rejected cannot ask again. Brand owners (`creator:manage`) decide requests at `/home/<org>/creators/requests`. Members see an explanation instead of the controls. Approving a removed creator is refused until the brand restores them. Each decision is audited (`campaign_membership.approve|reject`). — Admission is separate from post review. — production-requirements.md §2.
- CREATOR-PORTAL-08 — Creators see only the **published** revision (`playbook_revisions` at `playbooks.published_revision`), never the working draft. Variation is deterministic: FNV-1a of `creatorId|playbookId|revision|slot` picks one on-screen text per scene (UGC), one image and one caption per slide (Slideshow), and one description. Hashtags are the revision's tags followed by `#primary`, deduplicated. A new revision may reshuffle. — Stable per creator, spread across creators. — reference-build-specification.md L05–L07; production-requirements.md §2.
- CREATOR-PORTAL-09 — Submission rules: approved admission, active campaign, a distributed playbook of that campaign, and one of the creator's own accounts on a campaign platform. The URL must be https on that platform's own hosts, with no credentials or port. It is normalized (fragment and query dropped except YouTube `v`, trailing slash removed, `m.`/bare hosts mapped to `www.`), and the same platform + URL is rejected even across creators. The caption must contain the campaign's primary hashtag as a whole tag. Posts are stored as `reviewStatus = 'submitted'`, frozen to the playbook revision. Paid-ad rights are an opt-in checkbox, unticked by default, with the terms shown beside it. Opting in writes `ad_rights_grants` (providers: TikTok → `tiktok_ads`; Instagram/Facebook Reels → `meta_ads`; none for YouTube/Snapchat), `evidence_kind = creator_portal_consent`, an evidence reference that names the terms version, and an expiry 180 days after the grant. — Rights need explicit, scoped, time-limited consent. — ext-ads.ts (ADS-02/ADS-03); production-requirements.md §2.
- CREATOR-PORTAL-10 — Earnings read only `ledger_entries` in the `creator_obligation` account that reference the creator (`ref_type='creator'`) or one of their posts (`ref_type='post'`). With no entries the portal shows a confirmed $0.00 and explains that earnings start once a post is approved, its views are verified and the campaign's earning policy is configured. No estimates are shown. Payouts show "not configured" while `capability('creator_payouts')` is unconfigured. — No earning policy exists yet (OPS-09). — production-requirements.md "Review, metric ingestion, and earnings".
- CREATOR-PORTAL-11 — Social accounts are always created `unverified`; nothing in this area marks one verified. Handles are stored lowercased without '@' and are unique per platform across all creators. Each creator can link at most 10 accounts. An account that has submissions can't be removed, so brands can still trace those posts. — Ownership proof needs provider OAuth that isn't configured. — production-requirements.md §2.
- CREATOR-PORTAL-12 — The avatar shows initials only. Photo upload isn't offered because media storage isn't provisioned. Brand reference media without an http(s) URL are shown as "file not available". — Honest states. — readiness `media_storage`.
- CREATOR-PORTAL-13 — The portal uses the dashboard palette with a top bar and horizontal tab navigation (Home, Campaigns, Submissions, Profile) instead of the 256px sidebar. Creators use a few pages, mostly on phones. — Spec has no observed creator UI. — production-ui-plan.md (mobile, a11y).
- CREATOR-PORTAL-14 — No new tables: `ext-creator-portal.ts` stays empty. Admission uses `campaign_memberships`/`org_creators`, submissions use `posts`, rights use `ad_rights_grants` and earnings use `ledger_entries`. — Avoid schema churn. — CLAUDE.md "Database".
