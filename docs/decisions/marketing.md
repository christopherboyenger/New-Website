# Marketing and acquisition decisions

Format: `MARKETING-NN — decision — rationale — spec reference`

- MARKETING-01 — Funnel step 1 is a "Work email" step (any valid address is accepted; personal domains are not blocked). The email starts the lead and is carried into self-serve sign-up as `?email=`. — Step 1 was never observed; a single email field is the least-invented way to identify a lead before step 2, and blocking free-mail domains would be an unverified qualification rule. — build spec "Lead modal: step 2 of 3" (Step 1 is absent); evidence audit item 6.
- MARKETING-02 — Every step-2 field except "Anything else?" is required. Phone accepts digits, spaces, `+ ( ) . -` with 7–15 digits; website accepts a bare domain or http(s) URL. Budget bands are stored as the exact displayed labels. — A02 marks only notes as optional and shows no phone/URL rules; the formats are proposed. — build spec A02 table.
- MARKETING-03 — Job level options: Founder or owner, C-level or VP, Director, Manager, Individual contributor, Other. Job function options: Marketing, Growth or performance, Brand or creative, Social or community, Product, Sales or partnerships, Operations, Other. — A02 shows only "Select a job level"/"Select a job function"; option lists are unknown. — build spec A02 (`jobLevel`, `jobFunction` "options unknown").
- MARKETING-04 — A lead is identified by an httpOnly cookie holding its random ID (`lead`, or `__Host-lead` over HTTPS); the client never sends a lead ID. Resubmitting step 1 with the same email keeps the same lead (first CTA attribution wins); a different email starts a new lead. Steps are enforced in order on the server. Rate limits: 30 funnel actions per IP per hour (first `x-forwarded-for` entry), 5 new leads per email per hour. — Prevents one visitor updating another's lead and keeps attribution stable. — task brief; production-ui-plan `/start` "Preserve attribution".
- MARKETING-05 — "Talk to Sales" (`cta=hero_sales`) shows the same step 3 plus a "Book a call" card when `NEXT_PUBLIC_SALES_BOOKING_URL` (https only) is configured; otherwise it says the details are saved so the team can follow up — never that a message was sent. Choosing "Book a call" records step 3 with `routeChoice` null (the shared `leads.route_choice` enum only has `self_serve`/`learn`). — Sales routing after A03 is unobserved. — build spec "Routing modal: step 3 of 3".
- MARKETING-06 — The team notification email (`lead_received`) is sent once per lead, on the first completion of step 2, only when `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPPORT_EMAIL` is set; its true delivery status is returned to the caller and never shown to the visitor as "sent". — CLAUDE.md non-negotiable 2; DEC-004.
- MARKETING-07 — `/start` for a signed-in user: workspace members redirect to `/home` (L31); signed-in users without a workspace redirect to `/onboarding`; signed-out visitors see the funnel. Close and Escape return to the same-origin referring page (never `/start` or `/auth/*`), else `/`. — L31 covers only the member case. — production-ui-plan `/start` row.
- MARKETING-08 — Footer links only to pages that exist; Terms and Privacy are omitted until those pages exist (gap). Contact appears only when a support email is configured; Documentation only when `NEXT_PUBLIC_DOCS_URL` is set. — No invented destinations. — production-ui-plan `/` "working menus and CTA navigation".
- MARKETING-09 — While `commercialPolicy.decided` is false, /pricing shows "Preview pricing — final terms are confirmed before you're charged." The matching-credit offer renders only when `matchingCredit.enabled`. The plan comparison is derived from `src/domain/entitlements.ts`. — DEC-007 (conflicting reference terms).
- MARKETING-10 — Hero uses an original Chladni-style "resonance" particle canvas with a neutral eyebrow ("Creator marketing platform") and headline, no creator portrait/name, no frequency readout and no market claims. Product previews are non-numeric diagrams labelled "Illustration of the interface. Not real data." Integrations are text badges only. — Reference portraits, rotating names and "most-downloaded" copy are unverified marketing and rights-restricted. — production-ui-plan "Asset requirements and provenance"; evidence audit item 3; DEC-001.
- MARKETING-11 — `robots.ts` also disallows `/start` (a funnel, not content) in addition to `/home`, `/auth`, `/onboarding`, `/api`, `/dev`, `/invitations`; the sitemap lists `/`, `/pricing`, `/how-it-works`, `/creators`. — Keeps attribution URLs out of search indexes. — task brief SEO.
- MARKETING-12 — Step 3 card copy says "go live when you're ready" instead of "go live today". — Going live requires a payment method and publish confirmation; "today" would be an unsupported promise. — A03; commercial-policy `publishing.requiresPaymentMethod`.
